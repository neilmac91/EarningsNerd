# Brand audit: EarningsNerd as it is (2026-10-11)

(`https://www.earningsnerd.io`, the deployment of `main` at `dd7b9ce`) with Playwright 1.56.1 and
the preinstalled Chromium, at 1440×900 and 390×844, in both themes (`localStorage.theme` set
before load, as the app's pre-paint script reads it), on a first visit with the cookie bar left
where a visitor meets it. Captures are downscaled JPEGs under [`audit/`](audit/); the originals
and the computed-style probes live in the session scratchpad, not the repository.

Scores and judgements here are the studio's. File paths are the evidence; code is truth

## 3. What is working and must survive

- **The evidence texture.** Provenance chips with the quotes glyph for a passage and braces for an
  XBRL figure (`SourceTrace.tsx`, `sourceTraceChip.ts`); bracketed citation markers `[1]` and
  `[F1]` in the answer register (`AskFilingDemo.tsx`, `CopilotMessage.tsx`); the tally sentences
  ("3 of 5 checkable figures matched the company's XBRL", "N withheld because the evidence could
  not be matched"); the one-surface hero example with a real evidence row headed by the filing's own
  words. This is the only thing on the page a competitor does not have.
- **The gates and the discipline.** The ESLint design rules, the dialog allowlist, the snapshot
  parity spec, the no-em-dash gate, the planLimits lockstep, the one access decision
  (`access.ts`), the rule that no placeholder testimonial ships. The brand work inherits these.
- **Headline A and the line "for investors who still check the source".** Variant A says what the
  product does and for whom in nine words; the clause is the audience in the audience's own
  self-image.
- **The name and the italic Nerd.** "EarningsNerd" promises a person. The italic is the one typographic
  gesture the brand owns.
- **Sentences that carry a reason.** "Summaries are generated once per filing and shared. That is
  what makes unlimited reading possible at this price." (`PricingSection.tsx`); "Start with a real
  filing. Read the Apple 10-K summary first. Then read your own." (`CtaBanner.tsx`). These are the
  model for the rest.
- **The filing page as a document.** Numbered sections in the data face, section titles with a point
  of view ("The Print", "Results That Matter", "What changed", "Forward Signals", "Notable
  Footnotes"), the identity strip, the Source pane named for what it holds.
- **The honesty markers.** "Full summary" and the quality badge, "withheld", "located", "checkable",
  "Cited. Open the section to confirm."
- **The instinct behind the quips.** A person at work, reading the fine print. The lines need a drier
  pass; the idea stays.

## 4. Facts the later phases must carry

- The live example's figures were re-verified for this audit against SEC companyfacts on
  2026-10-11 (accession 0000320193-25-000079; CIK 320193): `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`
  FY2025 416,161,000,000 vs FY2024 391,035,000,000; `NetIncomeLoss` 112,010,000,000 vs
  93,736,000,000; `EarningsPerShareDiluted` 7.46 vs 6.08; `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities`
  111,482,000,000 vs 118,254,000,000; `PaymentsOfDividends` 15,421,000,000 vs 15,234,000,000. These are the only sample
  figures the brand work may draw, until a refreshed snapshot is captured through the product.
- Prices are `PRICE_VARIANTS.control` $39 / $390 (`frontend/app/pricing/prices.ts`), with a $29 arm
  behind the `pricing-experiment` flag. The studio brief names a different figure; it is not in the
  code and is therefore not a brand fact; it is a founder question ([01-interview.md](01-interview.md)).
- Access is invite-only (`ACCESS_COPY.invite`); Free is 5 summaries a month, 3 Ask questions, 3
  earnings-day alerts, 90-day history (`frontend/lib/planLimits.ts`, mirrored from
  `backend/app/services/entitlements.py`).
- The summary schema's nine section titles are those at `backend/app/services/summary_schema.py:71-79`;
  "Segments" (`:77`) is in the schema but not in the live Apple page's rail, and "What changed" is
  added by the renderer (`SummaryBlocks.tsx:122`), so the rail varies by filing and the sales page
  must take its list from the schema with the renderer's rule, not from a screenshot.
- The hero headline is a PostHog experiment (`landing-headline-experiment`, variants A, B, C); A is
  in the HTML. Brand copy changes to the headline replace or extend the variants; they do not
  bypass the experiment.
- `?v=3` is the current OG query; Phase 5 bumps both references.
