# The mark (2026-10-11)

Phase 5 of the brand studio brief, under the founder's instruction of the same day: the logo and
monogram do not change. So this phase audits the mark as drawn, adds arrangements that reuse its
paths, writes the rules that were missing, and redesigns the social card. The geometry in
`frontend/public/assets/earningsnerd-mark-mono.svg` is byte-identical before and after
(`lessons/brand-the-mark-is-protected.md`).

## 1. The audit

[The board](mark/mark-board.png): the mark on a grid with its clear space, the bare mark at 16 to
120 px on cream and on navy, one colour on white and on black, the app tile on light and dark tab
chrome, and an embroidery simulation at 60 mm.

What the board shows:

- **Grid.** The E is three bars on a stem, the N two diagonals and a rising stroke whose head is
  the arrow. The bars are 11 units tall with 4.5-unit gaps in a 94.6 × 73.2 viewBox.
- **16 px.** The E and the N fuse into one shape in every colour. The arrowhead survives from 32 px.
  The self-backgrounded tile reads at 16 and 24 px because the squircle frames it; it is the
  rendition for every placement under 32 px (favicon, tab, list avatars).
- **One colour.** Black on white and white on black both hold from 32 px; the mark needs no
  outline or second colour.
- **Dark.** Light sage `#7FB295` on navy reads at the same sizes as deep sage on cream.
- **Embroidery at 60 mm wide** (rasterised at 20 px/mm; strokes measured with a distance transform
  along the stroke ridges): the thinnest stroke is about 6 mm and the median 9.8 mm; the E's open
  counters (the bar gaps) are 2.85 mm; the E's bars and stem are 7.0 and 7.3 mm. The only feature
  under 1 mm is the point where the N's first diagonal passes the E (0.1 mm): a satin stitch joins
  them, and the mark survives it (the simulation on the board opens and closes the raster with a
  1 mm element and nothing but the arrow's tip rounds). Minimum embroidered width: 60 mm.

Verdict: the mark passes every test the brief set except the bare 16 px, and the tile already
exists for that case. Nothing needed redrawing; the gap was rules, which §3 adds.

## 2. The arrangements

[The arrangements board](mark/arrangements.png). New files in `frontend/public/assets/`, all built
from the committed paths and the one wordmark rule ("Earnings" in the surface's ink, italic "Nerd"
in the accent):

| File | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `earningsnerd-logo-mono.svg` | The horizontal lockup in `currentColor`: one-ink print, watermarks, embroidery. The italic stays italic; only the colour collapses. |
| `earningsnerd-logo-stacked-{light,dark,mono}.svg` | The mark centred over the wordmark with one E-bar between them, in a 300 × 150 frame: social avatars, the business card back, merch, any square placement. |
| `earningsnerd-mask-icon.svg` | The mark letterboxed in a square, black on transparent, for Safari pinned tabs; declared in `app/layout.tsx` with the sage colour. |

The horizontal lockup, icon, marks and app tile are unchanged. The favicon, touch icon and PWA icons
were regenerated by `npm run brand:assets` and came back byte-identical, which is the pipeline's
proof that the mark did not move.

## 3. The rules

Added to `frontend/public/assets/LOGO_README.md`: the mark and wordmark do not change; clear space
of one E-bar (11.6% of the mark's width) on every side; minimum sizes (bare mark 32 px / 8 mm, tile
below that; horizontal lockup 120 px / 30 mm; stacked 64 px / 16 mm; embroidery 60 mm); where the
mark goes (top-left on every owned surface, stacked for squares, never centred as a hero); misuse
(no photographs, recolouring, shadows, outlines, gradients, rotation, or a different-size lineup
with a competitor's mark).

## 4. The social card

`frontend/scripts/brand/og-template.html` now carries the device and the promise: the lockup
top-left, the headline "The summary that shows its work." with the mark (the highlight) under the
phrase, and beneath it a marked line: a margin with `[F1]`, the sentence "Total net sales rose 6.4%
to $416.2B [F1]", the `{ } Revenue · SEC XBRL` chip and the filing's identity. The figure is the
verified FY2025 value ([00-audit.md](00-audit.md) §4). Geist Mono joined the committed render fonts
(`scripts/brand/fonts/GeistMono-latin.woff2`, OFL, the same file the PDF export embeds) so the data
face renders deterministically. The card's URL, with its `?v=` cache-buster (now `?v=5`), is typed once, in `lib/socialCard.ts` (D-038).
