## Design Specificity Verdict: mixed

**LLM assessment (A, unanchored).** The evidence layer is unmistakably this product: one provenance chip recipe reused on metrics, risks, quotes, footnotes and answers with an honest vocabulary ("Verified in filing", "Cited", "Revenue · SEC XBRL", "Source match found" plus "A source match does not verify every claim"), bracketed `[1]`/`[F1]` markers whose popover shows the XBRL concept, value and period, a Newsreader serif reserved for the filing's own words, mono tabular figures, and section names written for investors ("The Print", "Results That Matter", "Forward Signals", "What changed"). None of that could be lifted into an unrelated SaaS. The shell around it could: a cream card stack with a sticky TOC, a chat rail docked right with starter prompts and a blurred upsell teaser, a sparkle launcher with a coachmark, Next's default 404, and a cookie bar that outranks the research chrome. ## Overall impression

The product's signature, evidence you can inspect, is real and consistently rendered: every number and quote carries the same calm chip, the answer cites its sources with scoped language, and the filing's own words get their own typographic voice. The single biggest opportunity is that the verify step of the research task does not complete from the summary. In production there is no in-app filing text, and clicking or tapping "Verified in filing" opens nothing at all; the only route to the source is a hover popover whose EDGAR link keyboard and touch users cannot reach, and that link lands on an EDGAR directory listing rather than the document. Around that, three plumbing problems (the cookie bar outranking the research chrome, the phone metrics table, and focus loss when the rail closes) make the first visit and the keyboard journey noticeably worse than the design intends.

## What's working

1. **One honest provenance texture across the whole page.** Metrics, risk excerpts, quotes, footnotes and Copilot answers use the same compact chip recipe and a vocabulary that scopes its own claim. The reader learns the pattern once (`SourceTrace.tsx`, `CitationChip.tsx`; `A-crop-anon-metrics.png`, `filing-light-desktop-pro-ask-flow__answer.png`).
2. **The Source Voice rule made physical.** When a citation resolves, the pane switches to Newsreader 19/1.7 with the exact span painted via the Highlight API and a flash fallback, so the filing's words are visibly a different voice from the sans summary and the mono answer. Fixture-dependent in this environment, but the code path is real (`filing-light-desktop-pro-ask-flow__filing-highlight.png`).
3. **State design that tells the truth.** Generation progress is derived from real backend stages with a live region, busy controls stay focusable with `aria-disabled`, errors get one card with a secondary Retry, usage is a plain count, the demo entry hides the quality badge on purpose and the normal URL shows it (`filing-light-desktop-free-genfail__error.png`, state matrix: 17 of 23 states rendered as designed; the rest were masked by server rendering or mock timing, not broken).
