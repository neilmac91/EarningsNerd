Where the vendored fonts in this directory come from. Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(OFL.txt, the text as Inter's release ships it). Each file's hash is on a line "sha256 <hex>  <file name>",
which backend/tests/unit/test_vendored_font_sources.py holds equal to the file.

Inter-Variable-symbols.woff2, Inter-Variable-Italic-symbols.woff2
  The marks the latin subsets below lack, roman and italic, for the PDFs: the dagger and double dagger, the
  won and rupee signs a summary figure may carry, the left and right arrows (the latin subsets carry the up
  and down ones), "almost equal to" (the 20-F and 6-K prompts ask for "≈US$X" beside a convenience
  translation) and the white bullet. pdf_branding.font_face_css declares both as the family 'Inter Var
  Symbols', after 'Inter Var' in every stack (a family of its own, since WeasyPrint embeds two faces of one
  family and style as one font), with unicode-range SYMBOLS_RANGE: U+2020-2021, U+20A9, U+20B9, U+2190,
  U+2192, U+2248, U+25E6. Each cut also carries the space and the no-break space (U+0020, U+00A0), which the
  range leaves out: Pango keeps a space between two marks in their run, so HarfBuzz needs one in this face,
  while fontconfig must never offer it for a space.
