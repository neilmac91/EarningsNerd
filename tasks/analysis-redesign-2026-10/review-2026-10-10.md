# Comprehensive review of the data-notes redesign (PR #1197), 2026-10-10

Reviewed as a product design director and an implementer, at the founder's request, after Codex's
review rounds. Method: six review lenses (design system, accessibility and interaction, frontend brief
against `main`, backend and exports, measurement and gate design, product and copy), each a read-only
pass over the PR's records and the code on `main`; every finding below was then verified by the
reviewing session against `main` at `af543b6` (items 1–9 and 11 of the brief landed in #1199, #1200,
#1202 and #1203 while the PR was open) before it was accepted, and the records in this PR were corrected
where they were wrong. The proposal's §11 lists where each correction landed; this file is the ranked
list for the implementing agent.

**Priority.** P0: would mislead a reader, violate a founder constraint or send the implementation down a
wrong path. P1: fix before the item ships or in the next PR on that surface. P2: improvement. P3: nit.
**Status.** `fixed in #1197` (the records and the prototype); `landed` (#1202 or #1203 did it); `open`
(a follow-up PR on `main`, item 10, Phase 2, or a founder decision), with the owner.

## P0

1. **The "passed" copy claimed more than the checks do.** A reported figure passes on admission from SEC's
   companyfacts (only a negative amount is rejected: `facts_service.normalize_companyfacts`); a
   calculation passes when its inputs passed with compatible periods and units
   (`fact_provenance.calculated_provenance`). The prototype said "The figure matches the company's XBRL
   as filed" for calculated figures and the strip said checks "compare figures with the company's XBRL".
   Fixed in #1197: §4.1 and §4.6 state what passed means; a clean report says "No figures need review".
   Open (`DataNotes.tsx`, `SourceValue.tsx`): adopt the two sentences and drop the sage "N figures passed"
   line; "Withheld" becomes "Unavailable" (§9, decision 9).
2. **Folding the Red-flag operands stripped every citation and letter from the reading line.** The
   detector sentences carry their citations only in the "Source operands:" tail; a fold hides the chip and
   the letter of a figure that needs review, which the founder's "never hide a note" forbids. Fixed in
   #1197 (§4.8, prototype): only lists of more than three figures fold, shorter lists stay inline in
   display form with their chips, and a fold's summary names the letters its figures carry. Open
   (`NarrativePane.tsx`, landed in #1203 folding every list): the three-figure rule and the summary letters.
3. **Item 10 could not run in CI.** `ci.yml`'s e2e build and start set only the API URL and
   `WAITLIST_MODE`; `app/analysis/layout.tsx` answers 404 without `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_ANALYSIS`; a skip
   guard would make the rule-12 gate a no-op, and the flag edit puts that PR in the high tier (it also
   shows the analysis links in the Header, Footer, ProDepth and the watchlist for every spec). Fixed in
   #1197: §7 item 10 is a complete gate specification (routes on `API_ORIGIN`, fixtures from the production
   capture, the matrix, the assertions, the committed `text-nowrap` mutation). Open: item 10, high tier.
4. **The measurement probes were blind, and three claims rested on them.** Element boxes pass while text
   spills; `scrollWidth` never counts a right-aligned overflow; the occlusion probe read one row at the far
   right and had never fired on any build; 200% text was measured at 1480 only; the current-build KPI
   probe found no cards on `main` after #1200. Fixed in #1197: both harnesses and the prototype's `measure`
   read text-node rectangles and `scrollWidth` per block, test both sides of each cell's content box, visit
   every rest position, assert their element counts, record their environment, and run 200% text at 1480,
   390 and 320; the prototype run was regenerated. What the widened probes then found and what fixed it:
   four clipped YoY-fallback cells at every width and seventeen clipped cells at phone widths (two-line
   fallback, 0.25 rem phone padding); 55 / 90 px of KPI overflow and a swallowed scroll area at 200% text
   on phones (one-up below 17 em, §4.4; the metric column un-sticks when it cannot leave one period column
   beside it, §4.5). Open (item 10): the same probes in the gate; the `text-nowrap` mutation (vitest stays
   green, the e2e fails).
5. **Item 9 touched a rule-6 contract test** (`analysis-api.spec.ts`, the SSE parsing contract, pinned
   the removed "FY2022" chip) while the hand-over said no locked test was affected. Landed: #1203 changed
   that one selector as a PR-body-documented contract change at the high tier, which is the sanctioned
   path. Fixed in #1197: §4.9 and §11 record it. Open: none.
6. **The evidence dialog bound operands by position.** A margin's inputs are stored `[revenue, income]`
   while its formula is `100 * income / revenue`, so every margin dialog in the prototype printed a false
   equation. Fixed in #1197 (prototype binds by the formula's concepts; §4.6). Landed: #1203's
   `substitutedFormula` binds by formula id; the implementation must keep binding by concept and never by
   list position (test net margin, current ratio, FCF and a derived Q4).

## P1

7. **Phase 2 must not store `data_notes` on the dataset.** The snapshot fingerprint is recomputed in three
   places from everything but `snapshot_id` and `data_as_of`; a stored list changes `snapshot_id`,
   invalidates every cached narrative and makes the exports answer 409. Fixed in #1197 (§7 Phase 2 item
   1): exports compute the notes from the dataset they render; an API-shipped list joins the exclusion set
   with `test_quarterly_trust_dataset.py` extended. Open: Phase 2.
8. **Phase 2 ports `lib/dataNotes.ts` line for line**, not the §4.1 prose: unknown notes keyed by their
   supplied reasons, unavailability keyed by the reason set, "aa" after "z", missing periods as
   `not_in_dataset`, comparison kinds, annual counts, citation-only lettering; Python traps (JSON
   separators, truthy `{}`, `0` and `"nm"` comparisons). Fixed in #1197 (§7 Phase 2 item 1; the prototype's
   letters now continue "aa"). Open: Phase 2.
9. **`NARRATIVE_FORMAT_VERSION` has no column, and the dated detector text sits inside the fingerprint.**
   A new version column is a migration (high tier); rewriting the detector sentences changes `snapshot_id`
   and 409s every saved PDF. Fixed in #1197 (§7 Phase 2 item 4): bump `PROMPT_VERSION`, render at catalogue
   time from structured fields, derive `evidence` without persisting it, state the 409 and the regeneration
   cost. Open: Phase 2.
10. **The wrong evidence gate was named for the narrative change.** B1 scores filing summaries and cannot
    see the catalogue; the RUNBOOK's Multi-Period Analysis observation-selector gate is the evidence, and
    the fidelity scan fails closed after the paid call. Fixed in #1197 (§7 Phase 2 item 4; an offline
    whole-catalogue scan test). Open: Phase 2.
11. **Three reason-sentence tables disagree**, one promising a repair (`analysis_export_metadata.py`:
    "needs rebuilding"); `provenance.ts` still prints "Source check needed" in the dialog; "(marked
    Computed)" survives in `NarrativePane.tsx`. Fixed in #1197 (§7 Phase 2 item 2: one backend table shipped
    as `data_notes[].message`, `REASONS` as the fallback with a parity test, a spec that fails on
    `/source check|\bverif/i`). Open: Phase 2, with the `validationLabel` and NarrativePane strings on
    `main` as the first follow-up.
12. **The strip's locator was wrong and its resting line names no letters.** "10 year-over-year in the
    headline figures and charts": the charts plot values, and only the latest cards show a YoY change.
    Fixed in #1197 (§4.1 copy). Open (`DataNotes.tsx`): letters beside the counts; "24 comparisons carry
    note a: 14 quarter-over-quarter changes in the table and 10 year-over-year changes, of which the
    headline cards show one and the exports carry all".
13. **§4.6 omitted the comparison block** that `SourceValue` shows, so a figure whose own checks passed
    but whose change line carries a letter had no explanation; "Withheld" contradicted `DESIGN.md`'s
    "Unavailable"; a gap was called "this saved figure". Fixed in #1197 (§4.6, prototype: status before the
    letter, "Unavailable · note b" with "Changes that need this quarter are not shown.", "Carries note a:
    it uses …"). Landed: the comparison block (#1203). Open (`SourceValue.tsx`): the vocabulary.
14. **Annual mode, the teaser and the landing demo render the same table and were unspecified**
    (basis, window labels, the CAGR column's width and snap membership, print). Fixed in #1197 (§4.5).
    Landed: #1202 ships "prior year" and the window from N > 6. Open (`MetricsTable.tsx`): the CAGR
    column at the period width inside the snap set with its window in the header; the teaser and the demo
    without the window control; disclosures open on `beforeprint`.
15. **Home/End scrolled the page, and a region handler must ignore keys from the portalled dialog.**
    Landed: #1202's `DataTable` handles keys only when `event.target === event.currentTarget` and moves
    `scrollLeft`. Fixed in #1197 (prototype, §5). Open (item 10): assert Home/End change `scrollLeft`,
    never `scrollY`.
16. **The `Modal` focus list omits `summary`**, so Tab from the dialog's trailing "Technical source
    details" disclosure leaves the dialog (WCAG 2.4.3). Fixed in #1197 (§4.6 states the contract). Open
    (`components/ui/Modal.tsx`): add `summary` to `FOCUSABLE` with a Modal unit test.
17. **The sticky metric column turns see-through on row hover in dark** (`dark:group-hover:bg-white/[0.03]`
    beats `dark:bg-panel-dark`), which is the hidden-column defect the redesign removes. Landed in
    progress: #1206. Open (item 10): assert the sticky cell stays opaque on dark hover.
18. **200% text on phones was unmeasured and failed by construction** (cards 101 px wide for a 173 px
    figure; a 208 px sticky column in a 228 px scroll area). Fixed in #1197 (the two rules above, measured
    at 0 overflow, 0 clipped cells, 0 occluded positions at 390 and 320 with 200% text; §9 amendment to
    decision 4). Open (`KpiStrip.tsx`, `DataTable.tsx`): the 17 em one-up rule and the un-sticking rule;
    item 10's 390 at 200% text profile. Founder: confirm the amended decision 4.
19. **The picker's presets are a second single-choice toggle group** (`aria-pressed`, tinted) beside the
    mode `SegmentedControl` (one selected colour per group); the unavailable Quarterly segment uses native
    `disabled` (the house rule is `aria-disabled`); `Select` renders full width. Fixed in #1197 (§4.9).
    Landed otherwise (#1203). Open (`PeriodPicker.tsx`, `SegmentedControl.tsx`): plain `Button
    variant="ghost"` presets, an `aria-disabled` option with its reason, raw selects with `autoWidth`, a
    `role="status"` clamp line.
20. **Fixtures: the production capture is not state 1.** `meta-data-notes.json` keeps 10
    `legacy_calculation` values, 16 + 10 comparisons and 2 + 2 unavailability gaps after the repair; the
    prototype's six EPS gaps are not production; `source_conflict` has no backend producer
    (`source_check_needed` is the real non-legacy reason); `MISSING_ROWS` is a no-op on a fresh ingest.
    Fixed in #1197 (§7 "Not in this brief", README). Open (item 10, Phase 2): the gate and the shared
    fixture build on the capture plus the synthetic cases §7 lists; `prototype/data` stays a visual
    reference.
21. **The table's fixed widths clip without a formatting rule, and the brief stated none.** With 76 / 68 px
    columns the YoY fallback and long negative values overflow their content box. Landed: #1202's glyph
    budget (`lib/tableFormat.ts`) rounds a token that would not fit, and prints "YoY " outside the budget
    (that line wraps and misaligns its row). Fixed in #1197 (§4.5: padding stated, two-line fallback with
    top-aligned cells, the budget recorded as decision 6). Open (`MetricsTable.tsx`): count the prefix in
    the budget or stack the fallback deliberately; the gate's both-sided clipping and row-alignment probes.
22. **Warning ink on letters that claim nothing.** §4.2 put warning ink on every note letter while the
    design system and `DataNoteMark` use the muted pair for unavailable and no-record notes; chart letters
    in warning ink measure 1.6:1 against the honey series in dark. Fixed in #1197 (§4.2, §4.7, prototype:
    warning only for review; chart letters in the chart label ink as SVG attributes). Open
    (`TrendCharts.tsx`): letters in `chartTheme(dark).label`, never a CSS variable (the PNG export
    serializes the SVG in isolation).

## P2

23. Compaction can contradict a sentence's relation ("$12.8B was below net income of $12.8B";
    "1.00× above 1.00×"): relation-safe rule stated as new in §4.8, implemented in the prototype; landed
    in #1203's `narrativeDisplay` (keep its tests on 1.0033, 0.996, 1.00001 and $19.96B / $20.04B).
24. The region is a Tab stop even when nothing scrolls, the hint shows after paint, phones jump after
    first paint (`DataTable` measures in `useEffect`): tabbable and named only while it overflows, hint
    from the scroll position, measure in a layout effect. Open (`DataTable.tsx`).
25. recharts' `accessibilityLayer` adds an unnamed second Tab stop per chart whose arrows move a
    tooltip without the letter: turn it off or name it and carry the letter. Open (`TrendCharts.tsx`).
26. The comparison note is announced twice on `main` (the figure's name and `DataNoteMark announce`):
    keep it in the name, drop `announce`; give period headers an sr-only long form. Open (`KpiStrip.tsx`,
    `MetricsTable.tsx`).
27. Excel letters in cell comments stay hidden and do not print; "Q2 ’26" headers defeat typed filters;
    the PDF's compact money differs from the web's ("$1.23T", "$1000.0M"); the PDF letter must be `<sup>`,
    not U+1D43; the Note column is column 17. Fixed in #1197 (§4.10). Open: Phase 2.
28. The dialog's "exact 2.2255627095" is a float rounded to eight decimals; EPS in another currency shows
    "$"; operand units leak "pure". Fixed in #1197 ("As stored", shortest form, a unit map). Landed:
    #1203's `exactFigure`. Open (`SourceValue.tsx`): the per-share currency and the operand unit map.
29. Dashed bars read as estimates in a financial chart. Fixed in #1197 (filled bars, letter above; §9
    decision 7). Open (`TrendCharts.tsx`): keep fills.
30. "Q2 2026" hides the fiscal basis (a January year-end makes a July quarter "Q2 2027"). Fixed in #1197
    (§4.5 caption, §4.6 "Quarter ended …", §4.9 summary say "Fiscal quarters"). Open: the three strings.
31. The window control needs `fullWidth` at 320 (244 px of segments in a 238 px content box); the hint
    assumed a keyboard ("arrow keys") and never changed; "Last 4", "Newest 6" and "latest available"
    use three words for one idea; the table has no Notes chip; "n/m" is unexplained. Fixed in #1197 where
    the prototype shows it. Open (`MetricsTable.tsx`): the legend line and the chip.
32. The prototype's type and colour shortcuts (13 px and 10 px text, eyebrow dialog headings, the data
    face inside AI prose, a tinted lineage box, a hand-drawn chevron, raw chart colours) and its global
    `:focus-visible` rule must not be ported; `text-[11px]` fails lint (`text-data-xs`); `leading-[0]`
    and `z-[N]` outside `DataTable` need care. Recorded in the Astra prompt's "Do not port" list.

    at 200% text). Fixed in #1197.
