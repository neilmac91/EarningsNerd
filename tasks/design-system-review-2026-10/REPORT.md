# EarningsNerd design-system review

15 October 2026. Review only. Source baseline: `1f12fc835fcbf525042473ab0e988b4827734f38`. Public evidence: [earningsnerd.io](https://www.earningsnerd.io), captured today; its deployed revision was not established. Signed-in evidence: local production build of that baseline, with Playwright API fixtures and no backend or real account.

## Verdict

EarningsNerd already reads as a specific financial research product. Its document identity, source vocabulary, margin and mark, and separate data and original-filing type roles are working. The remaining fragmentation comes from shared contracts that stop short of their consumers: complete financial values collide at 200% text, sticky page chrome hides behind global chrome, modal sheets leave background controls active, and some page states omit their semantic frame. Repair those contracts, consolidate the few exact duplicate recipes, and finish the already-owned copy integration. The new sameness score is **12/42**, unchanged from the brand studio; the first craft score is **20/36**, where lower means fewer observable problems. The largest visual opportunity remains the founder-held landing composition pass, F6. No application implementation is included in this review.

## What is working and must survive

The six rules, Separate Signals, Actual Surface, Source Voice, Lifted Paper, Marked Copy and Calculation Provenance, provide a useful shared language. The margin and the mark connect marketing to filing evidence without adding decoration. Inter headings, system-first body, Geist Mono data and Newsreader for the filing's own words have clear jobs. Keep the cream/navy grounds, one sage accent, neutral financial amounts and separate direction/status meanings.

The filing summary reads as a document with hairline sections; phone metric cards retain complete values. Company and filing identities share facts and separators. Source and Ask stay in the Source pane, with source actions and the verification tally scoped to what the server establishes. Justification above 640px, the Analysis redesign and its data notes remain settled.

Modal, Button, RetryButton, inputClasses and the overflow-region hooks already own difficult behaviors: focus retention, busy states, retained failures, body locking and keyboard access. Consolidation must preserve those behaviors. Dark fill/hairline separation passes the measured ordinary text checks. The auth glass treatment is explicitly scoped in current source and DESIGN.md, despite a contradictory sentence in the implementation guide.
