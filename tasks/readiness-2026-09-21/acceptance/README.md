The JSON report names each missing or invalid prerequisite and reports separate `ready_for_paid_execution` and `ready_for_packets` values. Paid readiness requires a [pricing artifact](pricing.template.json) with exactly the budget ledger's seven fields, positive finite rates, the runner's model and base URL, an official source, and verification no older than 24 hours; positive available balance; and Fable model `cli:claude-fable-5-1`, contract `2`, and quota observed within 24 hours. It also requires a completed development smoke on a non-holdout accession and a reviewed request-budget-control commit. The budget form records the calculated full-program worst case. If that exceeds the $10 ceiling, a named, dated acceptance of the controlled incomplete-stop risk is required; a blank form holds the run. Packet readiness preserves receipts without making their freshness a new requirement after outputs exist. Neither flag substitutes for the runner's own atomic per-request USD reservation or verifies the human signer's authority. Unknown prices, missing source bytes, brief/attestation gaps and unavailable Fable all fail closed. The manifest's draft status text is historical; its accepted hash and the recorded founder decision bind the candidate identities.

Balance and Fable evidence must be JSON observation receipts matching the [balance template](balance.template.json) and [Fable template](fable.template.json). The hashed receipt must identify its schema/kind and observation time; balance requires `deepseek`, USD and a positive finite numeric amount excluding booleans, while Fable requires the exact model/contract and boolean `quota_available: true`. Inline prerequisite claims must match the receipt. Freshness uses the receipt timestamp; a stale but consistent historical receipt remains usable only for packet readiness. These checks bind claims to retained content; they do not authenticate a provider response or the observer. Before use, retain real balance/quota readback evidence supporting each observation and review its provenance. A completed template alone is not proof of a live readback. The source-binding hold remains unconditional.

After the acceptance runner has retained every output, preview and export, run `python -m evals.acceptance_executor collect --programme /ABSOLUTE/PRIVATE/OUTPUTS/programme --output /ABSOLUTE/PRIVATE/OUTPUTS/outputs.json`. The output must stay outside the programme directory; an existing destination must already be a collector index for that same programme. Before reporting completion, the worker hashes every declared artifact, including raw preview callbacks, into its completion receipt. The controller binds that result's SHA256 when committing the claimed slot as completed. The collector verifies the ledger-bound result and exact artifact digest inventory before materializing previews; later edits or missing legacy seals are refused rather than re-sealed. These checks detect changes against the retained ledger, not coordinated rewriting of the ledger and every artifact. The collector verifies durable slot and provider records, derives preview counts and hashes, and writes `outputs.json` containing `{"records": [...]}`; do not hand-author completion evidence. Each record has `accession_number`, `arm` (`candidate` or `comparator`), `draw` (1, 2, 3), `status: "completed"`, `error: null`, UTC `created_at`, `config_sha256`, `canonical_path`, `rendered_path`, `export_path`, `preview_paths`, `preview_count`, `previews_truncated: false`, `retry_preview_attempts_omitted: 0`, and `artifact_sha256` mapping `canonical`, `rendered`, `export`, `preview_0`, etc. Artifact paths are relative to the outputs JSON directory and must be unique, regular, hash-matched files without symlink/traversal. Candidate identities are all 30 accessions × three draws. The **comparator** is only H01, H03, H05, H07, H09, H14, H18, H23, H26 and H29 × three draws: 30 comparator outputs. Missing, duplicate or unrequested identities, errors, truncated/omitted previews, missing files or mismatched configuration stop packet creation.

The builder makes two independently shuffled reviewer directories with opaque case/packet IDs. Each reviewer receives exact verified source bytes once per accession, a source identity file, the frozen material-issue list, and all 120 output sets. Reviewer canonical JSON contains the six reviewed product fields from the production `Summary` row, including unchanged `raw_summary`; database IDs, timestamps and version stamps stay in the original custodian artifact. An unknown canonical shape or a remaining execution marker stops packet creation. Rendered/preview narrative bytes and export financial narrative are unchanged; the known export generation-date header is omitted from the reviewer projection to prevent execution-time leakage. Reviewer indexes and filenames omit arm, draw and configuration hash. The seed, arm/draw/config mapping, and original artifact paths/hashes live only in the separate custodian directory (mode 0700, mapping mode 0600). Keep that mapping outside reviewer access. The builder requires new destination paths and cleans up its own incomplete directories on failure; do not publish either directory until a custodian checks permissions and blinding. Source/company identity remains visible because reviewers must verify the selected filing.

The scoring and defect criteria remain in the accepted specification: zero S0/S1, zero fabricated quotes or misleading citations, at least 86/90 jointly ≥4 on completeness and usefulness, filing-level repeatability, complete same-contract Fable verdicts and human adjudication. These blank forms do not record a score or waive any of those gates.
