## The arc

## Red flags

- **Persistent losses (high, ten years):** FY2025's $460 million net loss is the window's smallest, at −7.8% of revenue [R1][F40][F50]. {comparison} *Analysis:* losses keep Snap reliant on financing, its stated primary source of liquidity [Q5]. {judgement}
- **Stock-based compensation (medium, nine years):** 17.1% of revenue in FY2025, down from 30.2% in FY2022 [R2][D390][D247]. {comparison} *Analysis:* it flatters free cash flow at holders' expense [D396]. {judgement}
- **Net debt (medium, three years):** $596 million in FY2025 [D304][D399], with buybacks above free cash flow [D402]. {comparison} *Analysis:* a new slowdown would now meet net debt, not net cash [D211]. {judgement}
- **Falling ad prices (medium, two years):** cost per impression fell roughly 10% in FY2025 on strong impression growth [P24], after falling in FY2024 as inventory outgrew demand [Q6]. {cause} *Analysis:* volume now carries advertising growth [P24]. {judgement}
