## The arc

Revenue grew from $4.86 billion in FY2016 to $14.3 billion in FY2025 [F1][F10], a 12.8% compound annual rate [F198]. {fact} The one spike was FY2021, when revenue jumped 89.1% [D241] and US sales of the COVID-19 antibody REGEN-COV, $5.83 billion [S48], supplied 66.4 points of that growth [D582]. {comparison} Revenue fell 24.3% in FY2022 [D292] because there were no US REGEN-COV sales that year [Q3]: the company had completed its final U.S. government deliveries in 2021 [Q1], and the FDA revised the antibody's authorization after data showed it was unlikely to work against Omicron [Q2]. {cause}

## What drove growth

FY2025 revenue grew 1.0% [D440] as two forces cancelled. {comparison} US EYLEA sales fell 42.4% [D577], taking 14.2 points off growth [D578], on lower volumes (competition, compounded bevacizumab, patients moving to EYLEA HD) and a lower net price [P10]. {cause} Regeneron's share of Sanofi collaboration profits rose 33.6% to $5.24 billion [D591][S52], adding 9.3 points [D592], on higher Dupixent sales [P9]. {cause} The business moved from US EYLEA, 63.0% of revenue in FY2017 [D554], to that collaboration profit, 36.5% in FY2025 [D590]. {comparison} Costs have outgrown revenue for four years [D367][D416][D464]: operating income fell 10.3% in FY2025 [D441] as R&D rose 14.0% to 40.8% of revenue [D455][D420]. {comparison} Diluted EPS still grew 8.2% against net income growth of 2.1% [D444][D442] because buybacks of $3.44 billion cut the diluted share count 5.6% [X137][D459]. {comparison}

## Margins and costs

Operating margin was 24.9% in FY2025 [F30], the decade's low [D749], against a median of 34.6% [D751] and a FY2021 peak of 55.7% [D750]. {comparison} In FY2025 R&D took 4.7 points off the margin [D465]; SG&A gave back 2.0 points [D466] because charitable contributions to the Good Days patient-assistance fund fell [P5]. {cause} The largest move was FY2022's 16.7-point fall [D305], when R&D took 11.4 more points of revenue [D318], partly because amended Sanofi agreements ended Sanofi's reimbursement of half of Libtayo's development costs and brought Regeneron's share of antibody research costs onto its own books [Q7]. {cause} Net margin held at 31.4% [F50], above the operating margin, on $1.65 billion of other income [X117] and a 13.9% tax rate [D427], which the company attributes to lower-tax foreign income, research credits and an IRS audit settlement [P2]. {cause}

## Cash conversion

Free cash flow was $4.08 billion in FY2025, a 28.4% margin and 0.91 times net income [F100][D425][D429], near the decade median of 0.88 times [D763]. {comparison} Capital spending rose 18.9% to $898 million in FY2025 [D447][F90], 1.65 times depreciation [D430], to expand research and manufacturing sites in New York; the company expects $1.10 billion to $1.30 billion in 2026 [Q4]. {cause}

## Balance sheet and capital allocation

Regeneron ended FY2025 with net cash of $16.9 billion, 117.7% of revenue [D434][D435], the strongest of eight biopharma peers (100th percentile; median −50.7%) [D780][D781]. {comparison} Return on average equity of 14.9% [D439] sits at the 25th percentile of those peers, whose median is 38.7% [D784][D785]. {comparison} *Analysis:* the cash depresses returns but funds R&D and buybacks through the EYLEA decline. {judgement} Buybacks and dividends took 93.3% of FY2025 free cash flow [D437], including the first quarterly dividends of $0.88 per share [Q5]. {fact}

## Red flags

- **Margin compression (high, four years):** operating margin fell 30.7 points from FY2021 to FY2025 [R1][F26][F30]. {comparison} *Analysis:* without renewed revenue growth, R&D growing at recent rates keeps pushing it down. {judgement}
- **EYLEA erosion (high, three years):** US EYLEA sales fell in each of FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 [D571][D574][D577], and combined EYLEA and EYLEA HD sales fell 26.5% to $4.38 billion in FY2025 [S45][S46]. {comparison} *Analysis:* the new formulation is not yet replacing what the original loses. {judgement}
- **Table data break (high, FY2018–FY2019):** recast and original revenue sit side by side, so the table's FY2018 revenue decline of 12.4% and operating margin of 49.3% [D92][F23] reflect a presentation change; as first reported, FY2018 revenue grew 14.3% [D771][R2]. {comparison}
- **R&D intensity (medium):** at 40.8% of revenue, R&D is the highest in the peer group (median 20.1%) [D420][D778][D779]. {comparison}

## What to watch

- **Operating margin:** a FY2026 figure below FY2025's 24.9% [D749] extends the compression to a fifth year; above the 34.6% median [D751] would mean product growth is outrunning R&D. {comparison}
- **EYLEA franchise:** EYLEA HD growth must exceed EYLEA's FY2025 loss of $2.02 billion [S42][S43] for combined US sales of $4.38 billion [S46] to stabilize. {comparison}
- **Collaboration profit:** growth above FY2025's 33.6% [D591] keeps it ahead of R&D growth of 14.0% [D455]. {comparison}
- **Capital spending:** the FY2026 10-K shows spending against 2026 guidance of $1.10 billion to $1.30 billion [Q4], after $898 million in FY2025 [F90]. {fact}
