## Corrections before automated resume

1. **Bind every saved or newly produced verdict to its intended input.** `tools/run_queue.py` skips existing slots on the first complete-looking judge object, without checking the complete row, identity or harness. It also copies declared hashes into its ledger without validating packet/request bytes before dispatch. `tools/e8_analyze.py` similarly trusts the first row and does not validate its loaded reuse mapping. A valid result from another corpus with the same ticker/form/draw can therefore be misattributed. The KO/E3 consolidator catches full-row mismatches later; the E8 analyzer does not. Require exact packet, original row, request and contract matching before skipping, dispatching or accepting a slot. The independent audit found no such mismatch in the delivered completed rows.

Refuse that route before a call, or use a separately verified wrapper that cannot omit guarding. E8 has not started, so no actual bypass is alleged.
