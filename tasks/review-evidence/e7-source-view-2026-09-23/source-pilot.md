# H29 source-method pilot outcome

The new review aid was exercised by two actual, fresh GPT-5.6 Sol contexts with no
conversation-history fork. Both roles received the same hash-bound source dossier and no
candidate/comparator outputs or one another's findings. Model selection is observed; the
immutable served-model build is not exposed by the dispatch tool.

Reference A produced 12 issue entries after all six readers, four source roles, five
submission members and both decoded images were reviewed. Its log accounts for every
reader line and matching file hash. An initial tool-output truncation was resolved by
bounded rereads; no context compaction or unresolved range was reported. Its unchanged issue
content is retained in an individual version-2 brief with a context receipt. Custody only
joins list-form limitations into paragraphs and retains original draft/read-log hashes.

Reference B produced 15 issue entries and ultimately accounted for all six readers and both
images. It reported automatic context compaction after the initial source review/draft;
the required direct standalone-exhibit read was completed afterwards. The draft correctly
records `context_window_truncated: true` even though it labels coverage complete. The
custodian therefore treats it as **partial and ineligible** under the current contract.
The original files and a separate status addendum are retained unchanged. A summary and
post-compaction reread do not establish full input retention in one context.

This yields one individually frozen A brief, **no complete A/B pair and no reconciliation**.
No global E7 readiness claim, holdout generation or Fable call follows. The 30-filing source
programme and its 90 candidate/30 comparator measurement remain open. H30 remains a separate
partial pilot. These are AI observations, never human or expert acceptance.

The next implementation is the proposed hierarchical source-evidence contract: bounded
source-unit reviews, unique actual contexts, complete document/modality accounting and
explicit preservation of every issue through per-filing reduction/reconciliation. It must
be reviewed and frozen before use; the B pilot must not be retroactively relabelled as a
valid hierarchy. H01/H02/H25 remain capacity cases. The existing source view is a building
block, not that implementation.

[Compact custody/status receipt](source-pilot-status.json) binds the local original artifacts.
Full financial issue briefs remain in the retained local evidence, separate from generator
or output-review inputs. Byte/range checks establish recorded coverage, not private model
attention, complete browser rendering, independent verification of company disclosures or
semantic infallibility.

The [independent custody review](custody-review.md) verified unchanged A issues, actual schema checks, all A reader ranges/hashes and B’s ineligible status; it found no custody/reporting blocker. It did not independently rejudge financial conclusions.

The [next implementation contract proposal](../../readiness-2026-09-21/acceptance/source-review-hierarchy-proposal.md) identifies validator/inventory entry points, source-fragment and issue-union invariants, compatibility boundaries and eight acceptance cases. It remains unimplemented and unfrozen.
