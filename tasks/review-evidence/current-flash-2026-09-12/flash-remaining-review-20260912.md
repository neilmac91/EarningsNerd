Refutation 1: read the apparently supporting “Total debt” row in its enclosing long-term debt table (WMT lines 944–982), which resolves its scope. Refutation 2: independently checked the long-term rollforward at 1685–1703 and separate short-term balance-sheet row at 248/description at 920. The omission survives both; run 0 even contradicts its own components.

**M2 — ASML run 1 similarly omits commercial paper from total debt.** `balance_sheet_liquidity.leverage`: “Total debt declined to EUR 3,699.2M from EUR 4,687.6M.” ASML lines 5612–5620 identify those as long-term debt before current/non-current split.
