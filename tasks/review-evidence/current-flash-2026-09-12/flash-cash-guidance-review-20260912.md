## Verdict

The baseline still produces material narrative errors on correct underlying numbers. Five clear must-fix issue families survive in six outcomes across five issuers. ## Must-fix survivors

1. **XOM run 1: blanket quantitative-guidance denial contradicts a supplied quantified target.** `forward_signals.guidance` says “No explicit quantitative guidance was provided”. Source line 1835 states expected Permian production of approximately 2.5 Moebd by 2030; line 1805 additionally gives a $20 billion structural-cost reduction objective by 2030. The same candidate includes the production target in its forward-looking quotation. A reader is incorrectly told quantified forward expectations do not exist. **Refutation 1:** checked whether the target was only an inference or historical performance; the source explicitly says “expects” and supplies a future date. **Refutation 2:** checked whether the denial was restricted to revenue/EPS guidance; it is unqualified, unlike run 0's narrower earnings/revenue wording. Survives. Preserve the quantified operating target and restrict any financial-guidance absence claim to its actual scope.

2. **F run 1: issuer adjusted FCF falsely explained by ordinary OCF-minus-capex.** `value_drivers.capital_allocation` says $2,376M capex exceeded $1,316M OCF, “producing negative Company adjusted free cash flow of $1.9B.” Source lines 278/282 give the two cash-flow rows; lines 1657 and 2159 separately report issuer adjusted FCF of negative $1.9B. The arithmetic gives negative $1.060B, not negative $1.9B. **Refutation 1:** independently recomputed subtraction and rounding; a $840M gap cannot be rounding. **Refutation 2:** checked if the issuer amount itself was absent or erroneous; it is present and correctly reproduced in the candidate's other section, while its cash-conversion sentence reports the different derived negative $1.1B. The failure is the asserted basis/causal connection. Survives. Separate and label the two measures; do not invent a reconciliation when the supplied bridge is incomplete.

3. **SE run 1: non-operating items included in operating income.** `earnings_quality.operating_vs_one_time` says operating income of $2.0B “included” $43.4M net investment loss and $21.0M debt-extinguishment gain (and similarly the prior-year items). Source lines 1164–1286 place operating income of $1,985.306M above those items and pretax income of $2,280.859M below them. **Refutation 1:** inspected statement placement, not just the labels: the items are below the operating subtotal. **Refutation 2:** independently checked the segment bridge at lines 1847–1853, which explicitly adds $295.553M non-operating income after operating income; 1,985.306 + 295.553 = 2,280.859. Run 0 also correctly calls these non-operating. Survives. Attribute them to pretax/net earnings, not operating income.

4. **PDD both runs: smaller revenue line declared larger.** Run 0 `the_print.key_takeaways[3]` says transaction services CNY214.1B “surpassed” online marketing CNY217.8B; run 1 `the_print.key_takeaways[0]` says it is “overtaking” the latter “as the largest revenue line.” Source lines 7539–7561 give 217,783,028 versus 214,062,685, in thousands of RMB. **Refutation 1:** verified same-year, same-currency, same-table categories; 214.063 < 217.783. **Refutation 2:** checked a growth-rate interpretation: both phrases explicitly assert size/largest, not growth, and even their printed rounded amounts contradict that claim. Survives in both outcomes. Validate comparative adjectives against their referenced numeric measures before publishing.

5. **MELI run 0: total loans called long-term debt in a headline.** `the_print.key_takeaways[3]` says “long-term debt increased 60.9% to $9.2B.” Source current loans/financial liabilities are $4,623M (lines 1865, 1893–1896); noncurrent are $4,570M (lines 1913, 1919–1922). Their sum is $9,193M; prior sum $2,828M + $2,887M = $5,715M. **Refutation 1:** checked that $9.2B was not a genuine noncurrent subtotal: it is the combined current/noncurrent amount. **Refutation 2:** checked other candidate text and supplied XBRL: the balance-sheet narrative correctly identifies combined debt, but the generic XBRL `long_term_debt` value is 9,193, demonstrating how an input label can propagate the wrong scope. Correct text elsewhere does not repair the headline. Survives. This requires source-backed debt scope; test whether #808's provenance changes resolve it rather than assuming they do.

## Should-fix survivors

6. **Both MELI outcomes prominently report derived FCF without the materially different issuer measure.** Both `earnings_quality.cash_conversion` report $10.8B FCF; both Prints also feature it, with run 1 explicitly labelling OCF less capex. Source lines 998–1044 define adjusted FCF, including customer-fund restrictions, loan receivables and fintech funding, and report $1.481B; the derived number uses 12,116 − 1,343 = 10,773M. **Refutation 1:** checked arithmetic and explicit basis: run 1's subtraction is valid, so this is not a fabricated $10.8B or an automatic prohibition on derived metrics. **Refutation 2:** searched the complete raw outcomes for the issuer measure/reconciliation and checked source availability: the $1.481B measure and its economically important restrictions are supplied but omitted. Survives as a material analytical omission/basis presentation problem, ranked should-fix because the explicitly derived version is numerically defensible. Present both with distinct provenance and explain that gross operating cash less selected capex is not necessarily discretionary fintech cash.

7. **PDD both runs: increased share count said to offset an EPS decline.** Both `results_that_matter.table[3].commentary` say lower net income was partially offset by a higher weighted-average share count. Source lines 9102–9111 show basic shares rising from 5,536,049 to 5,590,930 thousand. **Refutation 1:** source EPS policy at line 5649 defines positive income divided by weighted shares; a larger denominator worsens the decline. **Refutation 2:** checked actual denominator direction and positive reported earnings, excluding a loss-per-share sign reversal or declining shares. Survives; replace “offset” with “amplified” if retaining the attribution.

8. **PDD run 1: cash “more than doubled” when it increased about 88.5%.** `the_print.key_takeaways[2]`; source cash rows at 2071/2074 show RMB57,768.053M and RMB108,900.587M, corroborated by current-cash prose line 1710. **Refutation 1:** calculated 108,900.587 / 57,768.053 = approximately 1.885, below two. **Refutation 2:** checked whether the wording referred to a different cash basket or multiyear comparator: the candidate gives cash and cash equivalents and elsewhere gives the same prior-year 57.8B. Survives.

9. **F run 1: overbroad guidance absence claim from incomplete input.** Its `forward_signals.guidance` denies full-year guidance without run 0's “in the excerpts provided” qualifier. Source lines 1555/1561 refer to Outlook page 53; the supplied excerpt ends mid-word at line 2181 before that outlook. **Refutation 1:** checked whether the supplied omission proves no guidance in the filing: it does not, and the source points to an omitted outlook. **Refutation 2:** checked whether candidate wording preserves that coverage limitation: run 0 does, run 1 does not. Survives as unsupported scope, not as proof from these excerpts that a particular full-year number was denied. Acquire the relevant source or state the coverage limitation.

A related recurring presentation issue remains in `earnings_quality.cash_conversion` for F, XOM, SE and JD: bare “free cash flow” labels for the generic OCF-minus-capex result. Arithmetic is consistent with their supplied inputs; this review does not invent an issuer convention for them. MELI supplies direct evidence of why the distinction matters.

## Implementation implications

Prioritise numeric relationship checks (comparative size, doubling and EPS denominator direction), statement-subtotal attribution and explicit source-backed measure scope. Require a guidance-absence statement to reflect actual excerpt coverage; a quantified forward target must defeat an unqualified absence assertion. Preserve deterministic and model-authored field ownership when locating fixes: final-output defects are not automatically evidence that the model wrote the offending text.

The cash-flow display should distinguish issuer adjusted FCF from the product's computed OCF-minus-selected-capex measure. MELI requires restriction/loan-funding context, and Ford needs two clearly separated figures. Reassess these exact cases after the planned provenance work; do not spend another paid corpus run merely to rediscover deterministic output defects. No claim is made here about a fix, gate, deployment, or all production filings.
